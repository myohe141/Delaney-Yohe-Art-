About Delaney

Delaney McRitchie is a contemporary visual artist who blends fine studio art and ceramic sculpture to capture abstract expressions of nature, space, and texture.

Earning her Bachelor of Fine Arts (BFA) degree with an immersive studio experience, she translates organic landscapes and tactile materials into captivating glazed ceramics and bold canvases. Her creative process is deeply rooted in experimentation, exploring form, volume, and depth across mediums.

Artist Statement

"My practice explores the tension between fragile surfaces and heavy structures. Whether molding clay or building layers on canvas, I focus on the dialogue between human handiwork and the natural erosion of raw materials. Each piece is a sensory exploration of volume and balance."
